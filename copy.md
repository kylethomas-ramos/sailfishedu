## Frequently Asked Questions

**My student already has a school counselor. Why do they need this?**

Most school counselors juggle 200–400 students. They cannot give your essays line-by-line feedback within 48 hours, brainstorm story angles with you one-on-one, or coach the specific voice that an admissions reader will respond to. This is built for the work counselors don't have the bandwidth to do.

**Do you write essays for students?**

No. Every essay is the student's own writing in their own voice. Kyle coaches structure, asks questions to surface stronger material, and gives line-level feedback — but never drafts essays for students. Admissions readers can spot ghost-written essays, and beyond the ethics, ghost-writing actively hurts admission outcomes.

**What if my student isn't applying to top-25 schools?**

The intensive is built for students aiming at selective programs where essays are a real variable. If your student is applying primarily to schools with rolling admissions or above 50% acceptance rates, the full intensive is probably more than you need — the Junior Year Roadmap Session is a better fit.

**Can we add more schools' supplements partway through?**

Yes. Additional supplemental essays are $300 per school beyond the first five. Most families add 1–3 schools mid-summer once the personal statement is locked in.

**How do payments work?**

The Summer Essay Intensive is $1,250 at engagement start and $1,250 at the start of Week 4. The Roadmap Session is $225 paid in full at booking. Payment is handled through Stripe.
